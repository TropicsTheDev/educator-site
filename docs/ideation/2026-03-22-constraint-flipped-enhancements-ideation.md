# Ideation: Constraint-Flipped Enhancements

## Grounding Context
- **Stack:** Nuxt 4, Sanity Studio v3, PrimeVue (unstyled), Tailwind CSS 4.
- **Identity:** BJJ & Urban Kiz coach using PCT (Perceptual Control Theory) and CLA (Constraints-Led Approach).
- **Strategy:** Discipline-centric navigation (BJJ vs. Dance); theory as the "how"; conversion-focused.
- **UI:** "Classy but Playful" with a tetradic color palette (Purple, Gold, Green, Orange).
- **External Context:** Skill Trees (8BitJJ), Active Recall (Submeta), Task Constraints (Greg Souders), Affordance Heatmaps, Disturbance Sliders, AI Pose Feedback.

## Ranked Ideas

### 1. The Audio-Proprioception Script (No Video)
**Description:** High-fidelity audio guides for solo movement drills that use spatial cues and internal narrative (e.g., "Imagine your center of mass moving 2 inches left") to teach positioning without a screen.
**Warrant:** `reasoned:` PCT teaches that we control input (perceptions), not output. Audio forces the student to "feel" the perception change rather than mimic a visual model.
**Rationale:** Breaks the video dependency and builds deep internal awareness, centering the theory as the "how" of mastery.
**Downsides:** Higher barrier to entry for beginners who lack a mental model of the positions.
**Confidence:** 85%
**Complexity:** Low
**Status:** Unexplored

### 2. The Peer-Led Skill RPG (No Instructor)
**Description:** A 8BitJJ-style skill tree where progression is unlocked via peer-to-peer verification. Students submit "Evidence of Affordance" (e.g., a 10-second clip of a specific task outcome) and other students vote to "verify" the skill.
**Warrant:** `external:` 8BitJJ RPG mechanics + Wikipedia-style peer review.
**Rationale:** Scales coaching beyond the individual instructor while building a community of "checkers" and gamifying the "playful" brand identity.
**Downsides:** Requires a critical mass of active users to function.
**Confidence:** 75%
**Complexity:** Medium
**Status:** Unexplored

### 3. The Principle-Only Blueprint (No Techniques)
**Description:** A site-wide navigation layer that removes all "Technique" names (e.g., "Armbar") and replaces them with "Control States" or "Functional Principles" (e.g., "Spinal Alignment Interruption").
**Warrant:** `direct:` The coach's PCT/CLA identity explicitly rejects "technique collecting" in favor of "iterative reorganization" based on principles.
**Rationale:** Forces visitors to engage with the *theory* immediately, setting the coach apart as a deep technical thinker.
**Downsides:** Harder for SEO (people search for "Armbar," not "Limb Isolation").
**Confidence:** 90%
**Complexity:** Low
**Status:** Unexplored

### 4. The Virtual Connection Slider (No Physical Contact)
**Description:** An interactive SVG/Canvas widget on the Dance page. A slider labeled "Disturbance" allows users to move one dancer, while a "Sync" line shows how the other dancer's "skeleton" automatically reorganizes to maintain connection.
**Warrant:** `direct:` The "Disturbance Slider" concept in Urban Kiz and the PCT "reorganization" loop.
**Rationale:** Demonstrates complex theory (PCT) in seconds via playful interaction, embodying the "Classy but Playful" vibe.
**Downsides:** Can feel like a "widget" if not tightly integrated into the conversion flow.
**Confidence:** 95%
**Complexity:** Medium
**Status:** Unexplored

### 5. The Random Game Architect (No Syllabus)
**Description:** A tool that generates "Training Games" based on student skill levels and gaps. Instead of a linear curriculum, it "architects" discovery by setting specific task constraints.
**Warrant:** `external:` Greg Souders' Constraints-Led Approach (CLA) which uses task-specific constraints to drive learning.
**Rationale:** Turns training into a "puzzle" rather than a "drill," making the site a recurring utility for active training sessions.
**Downsides:** Requires high-quality content authoring for the game database.
**Confidence:** 80%
**Complexity:** Low
**Status:** Unexplored

### 6. The Solo-Affordance Lab (No Partner)
**Description:** A "Solo Drill" library where every drill is framed as an "Environmental Affordance." For example: "The Wall as a Hips Proxy" or "The Chair as a Guard Obstacle."
**Warrant:** `reasoned:` Affordance Theory (Gibson) teaches that action possibilities are properties of the environment. A chair leg can afford the same training constraint as a human leg.
**Rationale:** Solves the #1 friction point for hobbyists (lack of partners) while making the coaching "always-on."
**Downsides:** Risks looking "unprofessional" if the photography isn't carefully staged.
**Confidence:** 85%
**Complexity:** Low
**Status:** Unexplored
